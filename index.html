BUSINESS CHALLENGES

企業有了下一個想法，接下來怎麼走？ You Have the Next Idea. How Do You Move Forward?

HOW WE WORK

曜昕如何工作 How We Work

從現況理解與商業判斷開始，將模糊的想法轉化為範圍清楚、可逐步推進並檢視成果的行動。 Starting with situation analysis and business judgment, we turn rough ideas into clearly scoped, actionable steps.

STEP 01

理解現況 Understand Status

理解企業的產品、能力、資源、限制與目標。 Understand your products, capabilities, resources, constraints, and goals.

STEP 02

判斷機會 Assess Opportunities

整理可能方向，評估投入成本、執行條件與優先順序。 Map potential directions and evaluate costs, execution requirements, and priorities.

STEP 03

規劃工作 Plan the Work

把方向轉成範圍清楚的工作項目、分工與里程碑。 Translate direction into well-defined work items, responsibilities, and milestones.

STEP 04

推進與檢視 Execute & Review

依合作範圍參與執行，透過進度與市場回饋調整下一步。 Participate in execution per agreed scope, adjusting next steps through progress and market feedback.

CASE 01 · 產品發展與商業驗證 CASE 01 · PRODUCT & VALIDATION 休閒／旅宿產業 Hospitality & Leisure

從既有產品與財務資料，探索第二產品曲線 Exploring a Second Product Curve from Existing Assets & Financials

背景Context
企業既有產品線營收結構固定，希望拓展新收入來源，但不願在尚未確認市場需求前貿然投入高額開發成本。 Fixed revenue from existing lines; wanted new income streams without heavy upfront capex before proving demand.
判斷Judgment
無須從零開發全新設施；透過重新檢視財務資料與既有場域資源，即可梳理出被忽略的客群延伸需求。 No need to build new facilities from scratch; reviewing financial data and venue assets revealed overlooked customer segments.
投入Action
執行財務資料與產品結構檢視、設計第二曲線新產品雛形與定價銷售情境，並規劃小規模市場測試方案。 Analyzed financials and product mix, designed a new MVP offering and pricing scenario, and planned small-scale market testing.
結果Result
完成新產品雛形推出，並於三個月內完成第一筆實際市場交易驗證。 Launched the new product prototype and closed the first real market transaction within 3 months.
角色界線：曜昕負責財務與資源盤點、新產品規格與銷售情境設計；客戶團隊負責現場服務交付與日常營運接待。 Role Boundary: Yao Hsin led resource/financial analysis, product spec, and sales scenario design; client team handled on-site service delivery.
CASE 02 · 商務拓展與夥伴關係 CASE 02 · BD & PARTNERSHIPS 餐飲旅宿與跨界品牌 Hospitality & Lifestyle Brands

跨產業夥伴關係與異業導客機制建立 Building Cross-Industry Partnerships & Referral Mechanisms

背景Context
品牌過去異業合作多停留在單次互相曝光或發新聞稿，缺乏能持續帶來客源的合作機制。 Past collaborations were mostly one-off PR exposures without a sustainable mechanism to drive recurring customer traffic.
判斷Judgment
應從目標客群的生活動線出發，將單點品牌公關轉為具備雙向利益與導流機制的跨產業夥伴關係。 Shift from one-off PR to mutual-benefit partnerships aligned with the target audience's lifestyle journey.
投入Action
盤點潛在異業夥伴名單、梳理雙方客群交換價值、設計具體導客合作機制，並陪同拜訪與推進合作條件洽談。 Mapped potential partners, defined mutual value exchange, designed referral mechanics, and joined partner pitches and negotiations.
結果Result
完成合作協議架構設計，並實際簽訂三間跨領域異業合作導客方案。 Completed partnership agreement frameworks and signed 3 cross-industry customer referral partnerships.
角色界線：曜昕負責夥伴篩選、合作機制設計、提案與協商推進；客戶負責最終合約簽署、門市接待與後續常態執行。 Role Boundary: Yao Hsin handled partner screening, deal design, and negotiation; client handled final signing and daily store operations.
CASE 03 · 政府市場與投標策略 CASE 03 · GOV MARKET & BIDDING 資訊軟體與技術服務 IT Software & Tech Services

政府採購標案評估與內部提案能力建立 Public Tender Evaluation & Building In-House Bidding Capability

背景Context
資訊服務企業具備成熟技術能力，希望跨足政府採購市場，但內部缺乏選案判斷標準與標案提案經驗。 Mature IT provider wanted to enter the government procurement market but lacked bid-selection criteria and proposal experience.
判斷Judgment
不應盲目投標或單純委外代寫；需先建立案件適配度評估標準，並在實戰中將提案邏輯轉移至企業內部團隊。 Avoid blind bidding or pure outsourcing; establish qualification criteria and transfer proposal know-how to the internal team.
投入Action
拆解招標文件（RFP）需求與評選配分結構、評估企業規格適配度、規劃服務建議書架構、簡報重點與答詢演練。 Analyzed RFP requirements and scoring criteria, structured the proposal outline, and coached pitch presentation and Q&A rehearsals.
結果Result
協助團隊完成首輪完整投標與簡報作業，建立企業內部可持續複製的選案判斷與提案流程。 Guided the team through their first complete bid and review pitch, establishing a repeatable internal bidding workflow.
角色界線：曜昕負責選案評估、提案架構指導與簡報答詢陪跑；客戶技術與業務團隊負責專業技術規格撰寫及專案履約交付。 Role Boundary: Yao Hsin led bid evaluation, proposal structuring, and pitch coaching; client team wrote technical specs and handled project delivery.
CASE 04 · 外部資源評估與專案規劃 CASE 04 · GRANTS & PROJECT PLANNING 研發與創新專案 R&D & Innovation

從企業實際執行條件，規劃可落地的補助與創新計畫 Structuring Executable Grant & Innovation Plans Based on Real Capacity

背景Context
企業有新產品與服務轉型構想，想評估申請政府補助，但擔心計畫書承諾過高導致後續無法執行或核銷困難。 Had a new innovation concept and wanted to apply for a government grant, but worried about over-promising KPIs and failing reimbursement audits.
判斷Judgment
補助計畫必須對齊公司真實的營運方向、人力編制與財務結構，先確認執行與核銷條件，再決定申請策略。 Grant proposals must align with real business goals, staffing, and accounting structures—confirming execution feasibility first.
投入Action
盤點政策方向與企業條件適配度、梳理創新商業模式、拆解工作項目與可量化查核點（KPI），並規劃合規預算配置。 Assessed policy fit, structured the business model, defined realistic deliverables and KPIs, and planned compliant budget allocations.
結果Result
完成可執行之創新計畫架構、預算編列與核銷準備規範，使外部資源申請與企業真實研發進度一致。 Completed an executable project architecture, compliant budget, and audit preparation guide aligned with actual R&D schedules.
角色界線：曜昕負責適配度評估、計畫邏輯梳理、KPI 與預算合規規劃及核銷諮詢；客戶負責核心研發執行與財務憑證管理。 Role Boundary: Yao Hsin handled fit assessment, project logic, KPI/budget planning, and audit consulting; client executed R&D and accounting.
